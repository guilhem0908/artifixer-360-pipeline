# Progress report — 2026-08-09

Source: [Gauvain 360 — Rapport complet d’avancement du goal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1he00eHXpC-MQVFM5b6ocItEHD3ytpLcNXr2wnSp8sNk/edit)

Objective: convert a pinhole video into a coherent ERP panorama video while preserving the original viewing direction and real observations. This archive preserves the report's technical content while omitting Drive-specific packaging.

## 1. Executive summary

The goal was not declared successful. The work eliminated several incorrect hypotheses, separated artifact causes and completed the most expensive ArtiFixer computation.

Three distinct problems caused similar-looking symptoms:

- phantoms and doubled contours arose from incompatible geometry, poses or 3D surfaces;
- black holes and missing regions came from incomplete view coverage or missing ArtiFixer predictions;
- cross-face breaks and temporal instability appeared when views were corrected independently and stitched afterwards.

The retained architecture kept a 3D foundation, processed fourteen overlapping directions with long temporal windows, assembled them in one spherical graph and reinserted observed pixels exactly.

## 2. Output contract

- Input: a conventional perspective video.
- Output: 1024 × 512, 2:1 ERP video with 154 frames at 15 fps.
- The source viewing direction remains centered at yaw 0°, pitch 0°.
- No obvious pixel mush, black holes, phantoms or duplicated structures.
- Circular ERP continuity, cross-direction consistency and full-sequence temporal coherence.
- Exact preservation of real source pixels within their field of view.
- Unobserved regions must be described as plausible completion, not photographic truth.

## 3. Cameras and view set

The original camera trajectory is the anchor. Virtual directions rotate around each source pose but do not replace its position or orientation.

Fourteen 110° perspective views were used per pose:

- horizontal: H000, H060, H120, H180, H240 and H300;
- upper: U000, U090, U180 and U270;
- lower: D000, D090, D180 and D270.

Their overlap gives the model shared context and avoids asking one isolated view to invent an entire region.

## 4. Failure diagnosis

### Geometry, COLMAP and phantoms

A 3DGS can encode several slightly displaced semi-transparent layers when image alignment, pose, depth or surface thickness is inconsistent. Novel views separate these layers into visible phantoms. The correct diagnostic is to inspect raw 3D renders before ArtiFixer: if the base is clean and duplication begins after correction/distillation, incompatible 2D pseudo-observations are the dominant cause rather than COLMAP alone.

### Independent ArtiFixer2D views

Two separately repaired views may move an edge or invent texture differently. Blind distillation then asks the 3D representation to satisfy contradictory targets. Concatenating cubemap faces is not enough; attention, ray geometry, pose information and visible overlap must connect them during inference.

### Coverage holes

A black region may result from a missing prediction or from a stitcher selecting an unavailable label. The updated pipeline audits spherical coverage before rendering and fails closed if any ERP ray lacks an active source view.

### Seams and temporal instability

Feathering cannot repair semantic differences already created on adjacent faces. A frame editor can also produce attractive panoramas that change geometry from frame to frame, resulting in flicker or moving objects.

## 5. Baselines and rejected directions

- **Raw 3DGS/3DGRUT:** useful geometry baselines, but some versions contained thick surfaces, duplicates or coverage holes.
- **Independent cubemap faces:** created edge discontinuities that late stitching could not remove.
- **Qwen Edit 360:** tested directly on ERP panoramas; introduced hallucination and insufficient video stability.
- **360PanT:** changed the floor and introduced a large incoherent rectangular region.
- **Fixer, VACE and generic ERP correction:** did not jointly solve geometry, time and spherical continuity.
- **Synchronized 21-frame pilot:** produced pixel mush. It demonstrated that synchronization added too late is insufficient; inter-view communication must happen during denoising with real anchors.

## 6. Retained architecture

1. Estimate and validate cameras from real observations.
2. Build the initial scene and an aligned source ERP render.
3. Project every ERP pose into fourteen overlapping perspective views.
4. Build a real-pixel anchor condition for each view.
5. Process directions jointly inside each temporal set.
6. Exchange information through internal ProPE cross-attention at each denoising step.
7. Use temporal windows of 77, 77 and 74 images to fit the available GPUs while retaining long context.
8. Assemble all directions with one global graph cut and multiband blending.
9. Reinsert the original pinhole pixels exactly at the center of each ERP.
10. Encode the final 1024 × 512, 15 fps video only after quality checks pass.

The fourteen directions were present together in the conditioning graph. The complete and recovery runs used internal cross-attention at each denoising step; they did not use naïve external latent or noise averaging.

## 7. ABCI execution and incidents

- **184318.qjcm:** failed before GPU work because Open3D was absent from the PBS environment. Open3D 0.19.0 was added from the persistent site environment and a preflight check was introduced.
- **184319.qjcm:** completed the expensive stages: 154 base ERPs, 2,156 raycasts, 2,156 projections, 2,156 real-anchor conditions and synchronized windows of 77, 77 and 74 frames. Final persistence hit a disk-quota error. RAM recovery saved 2,055 non-empty files; decode audit found 19 truncated files and 2,036 valid primary predictions.
- **184325.qjcm:** failed in preflight because an assertion incorrectly compared 154 sources with a 74-pose recovery subset. It was fixed before any denoising time was spent.
- **184326.qjcm:** completed recovery successfully in 1 h 46 min 42 s. It recomputed 148 D180/D270 images for poses 80–153 in one synchronized 74-frame window. All recovered 768 × 768 images decoded and matched the remote manifest hashes. Verdict: `PASS_RECOVERED_D180_D270_FRAMES_80_153`.

## 8. Stitcher hardening

- Pre-scan missing and corrupt inputs.
- Select only active views.
- Limit gain solving to available views.
- Preserve manifest labels through global graph cut.
- Remove labels that become unavailable during temporal repair.
- Fail closed on incomplete spherical coverage.
- Add explicit `--allow-missing-inputs` behavior while keeping strict mode as the default.
- Fix the active-view graph-cut position-list size mismatch.

## 9. Tests and evidence

Nine targeted stitcher tests passed, including strict rejection, controlled missing-input tolerance, graph-cut label exclusion, unavailable-label repair and detection of non-empty but corrupt PNGs. Python compilation also passed.

A smoke test inspected frames 0, 4, 15, 53, 77, 119 and 153. Real-pixel reinsertion had maximum error 0 and covered an average fraction of 0.0774002075.

The combined set contained 2,105 of 2,156 predictions: 1,957 verified primary predictions and 148 recovered ones. The 51 remaining gaps were isolated D270 views before pose 80. Every frame retained at least thirteen views, and a 512 × 256 coverage audit found no uncovered ERP ray. Verdict: `PASS_FULL_SPHERICAL_COVERAGE`.

## 10. Final full-render verdict

Global stitching produced 154 H.264 panoramas at 1024 × 512 and 15 fps (10.27 s). Coverage remained complete despite 51 isolated missing D270 predictions. Exact source-pixel reinsertion passed with maximum error 0.

Final verdict: `QC_FAIL_VISUAL_AND_TEMPORAL`.

- Maximum black fraction: 3.77%.
- Circular seam: 7.65× local contrast.
- Maximum temporal warp MAE: 0.1298 for a 0.10 threshold.
- Maximum temporal p95: 0.4196 for a 0.30 threshold.

Walls, furniture and structures remained strongly deformed. The candidate was rejected as a final result and retained only as a diagnostic.

## 11. Conclusion and next decision

The branch established a controlled procedure—overlapping views, shared denoising context, verified coverage, global assembly and exact locking of observed pixels—but did not obtain acceptable canonical geometry.

The same pipeline should not simply be rerun. The next method needs proposal → verification → commit semantics, or a depth/geometry-guided representation that prevents incompatible multiview repairs from being distilled into the shared scene.

Open limitations remain:

- missing D270 predictions reduce local texture redundancy even though coverage passes;
- unobserved content remains plausible completion;
- complete coverage does not guarantee correct geometry;
- a seven-frame smoke test cannot prove 154-frame stability;
- phantoms already present before ArtiFixer require geometry correction, while phantoms introduced after distillation require stronger multiview verification and selective commit.
