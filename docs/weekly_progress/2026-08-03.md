## Slide 1 — Framework overview

Pinhole video → geometry-aware restoration → equirectangular 360° video.

### Capture and reconstruction

Pinhole video → frame extraction → COLMAP intrinsics, poses and sparse geometry → posed real frames → initial 3DGRUT/3D Gaussian scene.

### Render, restore and distill

One shared Gaussian scene produces world-locked overlapping pinhole renders. A spherical-snake ordering provides stable directions and smooth turns. ArtiFixer repairs one common temporal stream, and geometry-constrained ArtiFixer3D distillation feeds pseudo-observations back into the shared scene with depth, overlap consistency and real-image anchors.

### Panoramic synthesis

Synchronized pinhole rendering → final ArtiFixer3D+ restoration → spherical fusion/ERP projection → 360° equirectangular video.

The method has no native spherical topology. Information propagates through temporal continuity, overlapping frustums, real-image anchors and shared 3D feedback.

## Slide 5 — Geometry-first correction

Lift RGB-D observations into one world frame, close the loop on stable scene structure, jointly correct pose and depth drift, freeze the scene and only then render panoramas.

## Slide 6 — Rein3D-style restore-and-refine option

Temporally restore radial panoramic videos, then feed them back into one global 3DGS as pseudo-observations. This is reserved for plausible completion after geometry validation.
