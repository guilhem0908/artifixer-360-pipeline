## Slide 4 — Frame-independent editing is not video restoration

Qwen produced 154 individually plausible frames, but each was edited independently and without temporal conditioning. The sequence was not coherent as a video.

## Slide 5 — Close the loop against a stable 3D world

1. **RGB + depth:** back-project walls, floor and structural edges into 3D.
2. **World anchor:** accumulate observations in one fixed world coordinate system.
3. **Loop closure:** align depth and image features to the same walls, trims and openings when the camera returns.
4. **Joint correction:** optimize poses and depth together, freeze the scene, then render every panorama from that fixed geometry.

Hard check: the staircase, wall trim and door edges must reproject to the same 3D locations after loop closure.

## Slide 6 — Rein3D direction

A possible later pipeline:

1. initialize an incomplete coarse RGB-D/3DGS scene;
2. render radial panoramic videos exposing holes and disocclusions;
3. use temporally conditioned panoramic video-to-video restoration;
4. fuse restored frames back into one global 3DGS.

The relevant idea is restoration plus 3D feedback: restored frames become pseudo-observations for one shared scene. This is a plausible-completion direction after geometry is validated, not a substitute for camera estimation or faithful recovery.
