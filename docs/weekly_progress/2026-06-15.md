## Slide 3 — NoMaD limitations

The tested model was trained on forward-facing views:

- it could not use the rear camera or drive backward;
- the goal image had to remain inside the field of view;
- in practice it required a trajectory of intermediate target images, not only one final goal image.

## Slide 4 — Habitat-GS and InteriorGS

The deck traces the asset pipeline from handcrafted photorealistic interiors and more than five million RGB-D views to lightweight reconstructed Gaussian scenes. InteriorGS contributes 1,000 scenes used by Habitat-GS.

## Slide 5 — Benchmarking 3D world creation

Proposed protocol: score quality as a function of distance from the conditioning camera path.

- On-trajectory: interpolation.
- Off-trajectory: extrapolation.
- Ground-truth metrics: PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS and depth L1.
- Reference-free metrics: NIQE, FID and MEt3R.

The deck distinguishes the proposed protocol from WorldScore by including reconstruction, ground-truth anchoring, on/off-trajectory distance profiles and non-aggregated diagnostics.
