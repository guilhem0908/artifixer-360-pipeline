## Slide 1 — How to compare reconstruction and generation

- Reconstruction has ground truth and can use full-reference metrics such as PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS.
- Generation usually lacks ground truth and needs reference-free metrics such as Q-Align, ArtiMuse, MANIQA, MEt3R and GSOQA.

## Slides 2–3 — MEt3R

MEt3R estimates 3D consistency between generated views without ground truth, using a pretrained DUSt3R-style model.

Average values shown in the deck (lower is better):

| Method | Average MEt3R |
|---|---:|
| Matrix-3D | 0.356 |
| HY-World2 | 0.259 |
| ExploreGS | 0.161 |
| GS-grid | 0.093 |

## Slide 4 — NavGSim

NavGSim extracts an occupancy/collision map from a reconstructed Gaussian field without a ground-truth mesh:

1. Slice the 3DGS with horizontal planes across the robot's height range.
2. Compute the analytic 2D Gaussian produced by each 3D Gaussian/plane intersection.
3. Stack the slices into a dense two-dimensional free/occupied map.

## Slides 5–6 — ArtiFixer

Input: a per-scene 3DGS/3DGRUT reconstruction with rendered RGB and opacity maps along a novel-view trajectory.

1. A bidirectional Wan2.1-T2V-14B teacher uses opacity-weighted mixing, camera pose, opacity, clean reference views and optional text.
2. The teacher is distilled into a causal autoregressive student that produces long consistent sequences from renders rather than pure noise.

Three variants were distinguished:

- **ArtiFixer:** render repaired novel views directly.
- **ArtiFixer3D:** distill the repaired views back into the 3DGS.
- **ArtiFixer3D+:** apply the autoregressive model again as a final post-process.
