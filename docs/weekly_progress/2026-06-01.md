## Slide 2 — VGGT-Ω

- Feed-forward reconstruction model.
- Inputs: images or video.
- Reported runtime in the presentation: approximately ten seconds.
- Dated February 2026 in the source deck.

## Slide 3 — LongSplat

- Unposed 3DGS reconstruction for long casual videos using MASt3R.
- Input: video.
- Considered especially relevant for outdoor reconstruction or whole buildings.
- Dated August 2025 in the source deck.

## Slide 4 — ExploreGS

- Enhances an existing 3DGS for explorable-scene reconstruction.
- Input: a 3DGS scene.
- Dated August 2025 in the source deck.

## Slide 5 — PanoWorld-LRM

- Generates consistent whole-house panoramas.
- Input: text, a 2D floor plan and a style.
- Outputs: panoramas, poses and room IDs.
- Dated May 2026 in the source deck.

## Slide 6 — World Labs / Marple

- Multimodal world model.
- Inputs: image(s), video or panorama.
- Period covered in the deck: 2025–2026.

## Slide 7 — HY-World 2.0

Tencent multimodal system for reconstruction, generation and simulation:

- reconstruction from multiview images or video;
- generation from text or a single image;
- panorama generation, trajectory planning, world expansion and world composition.
