## Slides 3–4 — Off-trajectory problem and initial pipeline

Initial pipeline:

Video frames → COLMAP camera estimation → Nerfstudio/Splatfacto → 3DGS reconstruction.

Observed limitation: video provides limited viewpoint coverage. Inaccurate camera poses can produce unstable Gaussian placement, which causes floaters, blur and unstable geometry in off-trajectory renders.

## Slide 5 — Transition candidates

- **MASt3R-SfM:** learned 3D priors provide stronger image matching and better initial camera poses.
- **3R-GS:** refines the MASt3R-SfM camera poses and provides a stronger 3DGS initialization before restoration.

## Slide 6 — LongSplat

LongSplat, presented as an ICCV 2025 method:

- uses MASt3R as a learned 3D prior;
- converts dense geometry into octree anchors;
- jointly refines camera poses and 3D Gaussians;
- targets long casual videos.

## Slide 7 — Benchmark

Thirty-thousand-iteration training with a 70/30 split.

| Method | Average PSNR | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| COLMAP + Splatfacto | 26.08 | ~20 min |
| MASt3R + 3R-GS | 27.88 | ~30 min |
| LongSplat | 28.12 | ~15 min |
